<p><strong>Frequently Asked Questions</strong>
</p>
<p><strong>Why do some sections become uneven over time?</strong>
</p>
<p>It was an intentional decision to build the Rail Corridor around some
of the mature trees in the vicinity so as to retain them as part of Rail
Corridor’s role as an ecological corridor. Over time, these tree roots
grow close to the surface and start causing unevenness along sections of
the trail.</p>
<p>This is why we monitor these areas regularly and carry out patch repairs
when needed to maintain safety while balancing the conservation of existing
greenery.</p>
<p>You can play your part. Stay safe and focused when hiking. And if you
encounter an uneven section or notice any other safety concerns, please
let us know via the <a href="http://www.oneservice.sg/" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">OneService app</a> or <a href="http://www.nparks.gov.sg/feedback" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">NParks’ feedback form</a>.</p>
